02 / A little about me

Good design makes learning feel easier.

I'm Akshay, a senior graphic designer focused on eLearning and training. For eight years, I've partnered with teams to translate learning requirements into ready-to-deliver visuals, from lesson screens and infographics to supporting animation and multimedia.

I care about the details that make learning materials work: a clear hierarchy, a consistent visual system, and a reliable process from first brief through revisions.
